No new requests were authored. The original v3 directory, authorship plan, coverage plan and preflight remain unchanged. Functional priorities, ownership/replacement exceptions, 272 positive product bindings, budgets and hard colors passed. The auditor identified 37 positive product bindings whose expressed soft colors were not offered: v3 assigned the first catalog variant as a uniquely preferred label without public preference evidence. This packet corrects exactly those audited bindings. `CORRECTIONS.json` records every changed binding. The originating independent report and its checksum are preserved under `provenance/` and in the correction plan.

For each corrected binding, every source variant satisfying the public hard requirements belongs to the preferred target set with equal mass. Its private per-product evidence policy is `no-offered-soft-preference-indifferent`. This expresses indifference among eligible colors, not an observed human choice. It is annotation data and must never enter the inference DTO. `preferredVariantIds` is the flattened union of these per-product sets; consumers must not convert a set to an arbitrary source-first singleton.

Functional grades remain fixed. `pairs.train.json` adds actual-field contrast receipts without rewriting the original pair annotations or requests. These pairs support mixed supervision, not isolated attribution of a model change to color alone.

`TOKEN_PREFLIGHT.json` is reused byte for byte because public requests are unchanged. Its 205–294 tokens measure the compact semantic public-context formatter, without loading a model. They do not measure the full catalog choice LM prompt, which adds catalog, system and candidate content and requires its own complete consumer preflight. No truncation was performed here.
